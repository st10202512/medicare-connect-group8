About HealthyLife Family Clinic

HealthyLife Family Clinic is a small private medical facility offering medical consultations, chronic-condition management, and prescription services.

Our new MediCare Connect platform lets patients book appointments, request medication, and pay online — reducing queues and improving the overall experience.
